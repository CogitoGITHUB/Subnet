#+TITLE: Outputs — AIU NOTATION
# One job: what exists at the end, what it is good for, and what is not shipped
# Status: filled 2026-10-16; plans stay general, not yet reviewed.

* What exists at the end
# one line per thing a user can hold or run when this plan is done.
  - The element language with its chosen mark and literal names, and the
    parser with incremental reparse and error recovery.
  - Literate code: named chunks, targets, loading and sending, with
    skip-on-unchanged by content hash.
  - The engine surface answering the editor through the boundary; the
    notation core hosted in the editor (the done-line share).
  - Rendering: plain and rich unicode; comments stripped by rendering;
    the optional graphics tier planned and unproven.
  - Outlines, tags, properties, tables, planning states and links as
    named elements.
  - The notation's kit — JSON surface, MCP server, skill with executed
    examples, read-only plugin.
  - The notation's own checklist after the split, starting from tier-2
    content.

* What it is good for
# the everyday uses, in plain words.
  Writing notes, plans and code-adjacent documents that the owner and
  agents read without a manual; literate files that load themselves or
  feed other files; one notation in place of Org, Markdown and LaTeX for
  everyday work.

* Not shipped
# things deliberately left out of this output set.
  Math, physics, units, figures, citations and graphs as working tiers —
  far future, plan only; LaTeX parity; a second syntax for agents; any
  editor feature.

* How the projects relate
# which project holds which, in one or two lines.
  - AIU-Frame is the boot source of truth (early-init and foundation-init targets); it
    starts the loader; the loader is the cyberdeck file plus its modules under loader/.
  - Discovery under emacs-cyberdeck/ runs by stage: S0 before S1 at boot; S2 units are
    left for first use.
  - The custom cluster sits under files/aiu-registration/cyberdeck/ (aiu-frame,
    aiu-context, aiu-run, biomechanical-input-interface), its own subsystem, not file
    handling; the desk lives under entering-the-machine/.
  - Archive chapters carry #+cyberdeck-source lines: split from one manuals.org, not
    written as config.

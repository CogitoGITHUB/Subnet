* Risks
# one line per risk: what it is and how much it can hurt.
  - Racket cannot join a no-config baked binary on this phone — it
    reopens the language-split decision; the highest-stakes unknown.
  - The Racket build on the phone is too slow for the dev loop — work
    mode turns painful (WHEN-ON-LAPTOP: heavy builds).
  - Android kills break the journal and holder promises — work loss, the
    thing rule 01 exists to prevent.
  - Neither agent adapter lands cleanly first (ACP unverified; Hermes
    untested under proot) — the done line slips.
  - inotify under proot misbehaves — the live audit stops being live.
  - Scope creep from porting the old config — months without a working
    slice (rule 11; the map's verdicts point the other way).
  - The graphics rendering tier is unproven — rendering ambitions may
    need the laptop.
  - Long test runs and multi-hour work do not fit the phone — pacing, not
    feasibility.

* First signs
# the earliest observable signal that a risk is happening.
  - The measured start fails or balloons; bake cycles grow to laptop
    scale; the replay test fails after a kill; adapter conformance
    cannot pass on the phone; watch events miss or lag; the first slice
    grows beyond the plan's steps.

* Shrinking it
# what is done about each risk, or why nothing can be.
  - The Racket-in-binary test runs first and is allowed to reopen the
    seam decision (rule 09 demands it).
  - Keep the smallest slice order; every phone limit is appended to
    WHEN-ON-LAPTOP.org the moment it appears, in the same commit.
  - Pin one adapter first; the second stays behind the same contract
    (agents/ design).
  - Accept that the graphics tier may wait; it is recorded, not promised.
  - Nothing shrinks the phone being a phone — plan around it, never fight
    it (standing WHEN-ON-LAPTOP line).

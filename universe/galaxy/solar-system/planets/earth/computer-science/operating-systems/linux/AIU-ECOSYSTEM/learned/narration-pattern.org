** The schema decides nothing; the narrator carries the story
# events = data; narration = the run's voice; waiting = its own door.
- What PM does: =events.rkt= is the schema and decides nothing; =narration.rkt= opens and closes a run and carries step, note, warn, skip and decide; =wait-door.rkt= is the blocking door — =wait-for= BEFORE the block, =wait-done= after, and =why= is never blank.
- The rule: one path for every operation's story (what, why, how, UTC, monotonic duration); a skip without a reason is a bug in the caller.
- Verdict: REUSE.
- Read at: package-manager/src/narration/README.org; plan/ARCHITECTURE.org (=narration/=).

** The console printer lives in one place, and a lint proves it
# Nothing outside the one printer prints to the terminal.
- What PM does: the console printer lives in =narration/= and nowhere else (D-036); one-printer-lint fails on raw printf/eprintf/write-string/displayln outside the two printers.
- The rule: if any module may print, the feed and the screen drift; one printer or no law.
- Verdict: REUSE.
- Read at: gate/check.nu (=one-printer-lint=); docs/LESSONS.org (the exit-code entry).

** The wait door is the hang detector
# A WAIT with no WAIT-DONE names the hang.
- What PM does: every blocking primitive passes the cancellation door in =machine/cancel.rkt=; the wait door emits before/after with why; the test runner prints TEST-START/TEST-END per file, so a start with no end IS the stuck file and =at== says for how long; no timers anywhere (D-015) — SUSPECT is a report, never a limit.
- The rule: make hangs visible instead of guessing timeouts; the timestamp on the start line is the diagnostic.
- Verdict: REUSE.
- Read at: AGENT.md ("Always use the shared runner", "Ground rules"); plan/DECISIONS.org D-015.

** Values first, effects at the edge
# The story of an operation exists before anything is touched.
- What PM does: a plan is an immutable list of actions; dry-run is the default backend and performs nothing; the JSON document carries =confirmed= and =applied= explicitly, so a machine can tell "would do" from "did".
- The rule: every operation narrates a record of what happened — and for mutations, first a record of what WOULD happen.
- Verdict: REUSE.
- Read at: package-manager/src/entry/documents.rkt (~line 337); 0.FOUNDATION.org section 3.

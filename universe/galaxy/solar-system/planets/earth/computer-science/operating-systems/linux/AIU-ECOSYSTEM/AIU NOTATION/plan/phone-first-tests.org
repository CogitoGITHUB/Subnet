#+TITLE: Phone-first tests — AIU NOTATION
# One job: what must be proven on the phone first, what waits for the laptop, and how each is proven
# Status: filled 2026-10-16; plans stay general, not yet reviewed.

* Proven on the phone first
# one line per thing that must work on the phone before anything is built on it.
  - The chosen mark is typeable on this phone keyboard, comfortably, and
    never collides with prose or tags.
  - Titles and paragraphs round-trip with almost no syntax.
  - Incremental reparse keeps a working page responsive while typing.
  - Literate chunks load and send into files, with skip-on-unchanged.
  - Rendering — plain and rich unicode — is legible on the phone's
    terminal.
  - The engine surface answers the editor quickly enough while typing.

* Waiting for the laptop
# what the phone cannot prove; every line here is also appended to WHEN-ON-LAPTOP.org.
  - The optional graphics rendering tier — terminal graphics are
    unproven on this phone (already in WHEN-ON-LAPTOP.org).
  - Heavy builds of the notation engine when they exceed the phone
    budget — appended when measured.
  - The tier-3 work — math, graphs, the advanced syntax — far future,
    plan only.

* How each is proven
# the single check for each phone-first item.
  - The mark — the owner types a page on the phone; collisions are
    checked against real prose and tags.
  - Round trip — parse, render and re-parse produce the same meaning on a
    real page.
  - Reparse — typing latency observed on the phone; no full reparse per
    keystroke.
  - Literate — a changed chunk rebuilds; an unchanged one SKIPs naming
    the matched hash.
  - Rendering — shown on the phone's terminal; the graphics tier stays
    recorded as unproven.
  - Engine surface — an editor session over the boundary, on the phone.

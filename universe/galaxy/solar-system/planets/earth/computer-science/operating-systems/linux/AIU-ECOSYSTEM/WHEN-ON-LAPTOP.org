#+TITLE: WHEN-ON-LAPTOP
# One job: the living list of everything the phone cannot do or can only partly do.
# Status: living; append the moment a limit appears, never later.

* What goes here
# One line per thing, the moment it appears. Every plan file that hits a phone limit adds its line here first.
  - something the phone cannot do at all;
  - something it can only partly do, with the part that is missing;
  - something believed possible but not yet proven on the phone.

* The list
# One line per item: what it is, why the phone falls short, and where it appeared.
  - Real process survival | Android may kill background processes under Termux and proot; the holder's promise must be proven before it is made | base commit, before any build
  - Heavy builds | Racket build entries in PM measured hundreds of seconds under one compiled-roots setup and seconds under another; long rebuilds belong on the laptop when they do not fit | learned from PM, step 2 will detail it
  - Long test runs | a full test pass of the package manager took many minutes of near-idle time and hundreds of child processes on this phone | learned from PM
  - Terminal graphics | not yet proven that the terminal on this phone can draw the optional graphics tier | planned, notation rendering
  - Multi-hour uninterrupted work | the phone is a phone; long builds and long suites are planned around, not fought | standing

* How to add
# Three rules, so the list stays useful.
  - Write the line the moment the limit appears, in the same commit as the change that found it.
  - Name what is missing, not just that something is hard.
  - Never remove a line silently; if the laptop stops being needed, say why on the line.

#+TITLE: errors — the error type, and how it is rendered

* What this area does
=error-type.rkt= defines =exn:fail:pm= and the kinds.
=error-message.rkt= renders it and maps a kind to an exit code.

* What it may depend on
Nothing. This is the floor: =errors= requiring anything is one of the two
directions ARCHITECTURE.org says must never appear.

* What must never depend on it
Nothing may require =entry=, which is the other direction that must never
appear. Every area above the floor may depend on =errors=; ARCHITECTURE.org
also carries a sentence saying only =narration/= may, and the two cannot
both be true. Recorded in =plan/OPEN-DECISIONS.org= rather than silently
picked, because which one wins decides whether the tree is legal today.

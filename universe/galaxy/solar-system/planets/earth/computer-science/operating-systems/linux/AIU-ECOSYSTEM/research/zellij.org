* What it is
# the project, its language, its health, and who maintains it.
  Zellij (github.com/zellij-org/zellij) is a terminal workspace in Rust, a
  "terminal multiplexer" aimed at beginner and power users alike. Its
  stated philosophy: do not sacrifice simplicity for power; good defaults
  out of the box. Features include layouts (declarative, for automation),
  floating and stacked panes, true multiplayer collaboration, a plugin
  system for WebAssembly plugins in any language, and a built-in web
  client so a terminal is optional. Healthy: 35.7k stars, last push
  2026-10-14.

* What transfers
# one line per mechanism worth borrowing, each with the reason it fits a program here.
  - Layouts as data: a workspace is a file you keep and reproduce, not a
    pile of keypresses.
  - Server/client split: the session owns the PTYs and survives detach;
    the client is a view. Our multiplexer wants that seam, driven by
    events rather than polling.
  - Floating and stacked panes: panes as addressable objects with a stack
    order — vocabulary worth keeping in the pane model.
  - Customizability under good defaults: simple until you ask for power.

* What to avoid
# one line per mechanism we deliberately do not take, with the reason. No code.
  - The WebAssembly plugin system: hosting a plugin runtime and ABI is
    weight we do not want; our extension path is MCP and skills.
  - The built-in web client: another surface and another network path,
    not needed on this phone.
  - Multiplayer collaboration: drags in networking and accounts before we
    have a single-user story we like.

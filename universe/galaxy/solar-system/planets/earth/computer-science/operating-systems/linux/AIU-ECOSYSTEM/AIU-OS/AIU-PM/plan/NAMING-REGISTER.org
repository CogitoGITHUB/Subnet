* Files moved whole and unsplit, and why

=backends/vault.rkt= and =backends/jobs.rkt= are on the gate's
=FILE_PARKED= list, so the gate does not run their tests. Their
stated reasons are a pathological 470s large-blob case and an
undecided worker-race design question, not "untestable". Both
move WHOLE and UNSPLIT: relocating ~2,700 lines with no
behavioural net is already the risk, and splitting on top of that
would double it. Recorded in =plan/OPEN-DECISIONS.org=.

* Identifiers to rename, and why each lies

| old | new | why |
|-----|-----|-----|
| =run.rkt -> run-result-exit= | =run.rkt -> result-exit= | the struct carries exit, stdout and stderr; "run" says nothing |
| =run-command= | =spawn-and-wait= | it spawns, waits and collects; the old name sounds like it only builds a command line |
| =mcp/server.rkt= | =entry/mcp-server.rkt= | "server" and "main" are banned and say nothing about the job |
| =cli/main.rkt= | =entry/cli.rkt= | "main" is banned and is a filename convention, not a job |
| =core/ui.rkt= | =narration/print-console.rkt= | ui claims a surface; it is the single printer |
| =core/scope.rkt= | =machine/directories.rkt= | "scope" is the concept, "directories" is the job |
| =core/lock.rkt= | =machine/file-lock.rkt= | "lock" alone could be the vault lock or the store lock |
| =core/vault-lock.rkt= | =git/vault-lock.rkt= | sits with git because it locks a git-owned directory |
| =backends/local.rkt= | =install/local-perform.rkt= | "local" as opposed to what? this is the one that touches disk |
| =backends/store.rkt= | =store/store.rkt= | kept: it materialises a version, and that is what store means |

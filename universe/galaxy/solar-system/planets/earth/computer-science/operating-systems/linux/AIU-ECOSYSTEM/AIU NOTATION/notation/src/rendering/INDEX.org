* Where it sits
# why this area exists and what it may use.
  - Rust owns the hot path, and rendering is part of it
    (rules/rule-09-language-split.org).
  - strip.rs is the **only** stripper in the package. plain.rs calls it; no
    other file decides to drop a line. Plain rendering hides start, end and
    declaration lines and keeps the content word for word
    (syntax/first-slice-freeze.org, rule F).
  - Only strip.rs and plain.rs are compiled today: src/lib.rs lists the nine
    Rust modules of the crate and these are two of them. rich.rs is the third
    file in this directory and the only one not wired.

#+TITLE: Rule 05 — Every action is logged with who and why
# One job: the hard rule and what it demands in this program
# Status: current; the rule text is the owner's and changes only with the owner.

* The rule
# the owner's words, repeated unchanged. Do not soften or reinterpret them here.
  Every action is logged with who (me, OpenCode or Hermes) and why, at intent level (not per
  keystroke). Journal and logs rotate.

* What it demands here
# how this rule bites in this program. No examples; the plan carries the detail.
  Session, pane and command records say who and why at intent level; the journal and logs
  rotate.

* What breaks it
# the failure this rule exists to prevent.
  An action with no who and no why, so it cannot be explained later.

* Where it is proven
# the checklist item that shows this rule holds; named 2026-10-16.
  checklist/tier-1 items 03 and 17

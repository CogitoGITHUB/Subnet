* Files
# one line per file: its name, then the single job it performs.

  - register.rkt — the one table: name to meaning
  - dispatch.rkt — a name and its body reach the right meaning
  - literals.rkt — literal names: what a name is, what it is not

* Where it sits
# why this area exists and what it may use.
  - Racket owns the meaning side (rules/rule-09-language-split.org). This area
    is the vocabulary: it says what a name means, and it is the only place
    that may add a name.
  - A name that is not in register.rkt has no meaning and must not reach the
    model; dispatch.rkt refuses it rather than guessing.

#+TITLE: one-way law gap — 34 crossings, one decision
#+AUTHOR: Shape
# One job: close the gap between the law and the tree: move the modules
# the lint names, or correct the law to the tree as it stands. Status:
# planned only; nothing here is built. Source: =plan/OPEN-DECISIONS.org=
# (the owner's pick); the lint prints the 34 edges =file:line= every run.

* Purpose
- The one-way law is the structure law; the lint prints 34 crossings
  on every run. A law with unpaid edges is a lie the gate repeats until
  someone decides: (a) move the modules so the ranking holds, or
  (b) correct ARCHITECTURE.org to the tree as it stands.
- This component is the closing move, whichever option is picked.
* Name and location
- name: none new; moved modules keep their names; the law keeps its
- code: the modules the lint names, printed =file:line= each run
- area and layer: the areas the chosen modules sit in; the fix may not
  add a new crossing while removing old ones
- may require: as the tree already is; the fix adds no dependency
- may be required by: as the tree already is
* Public interface, in plain words
- none: the proof is the next lint run: 0 crossings (move) or a law
  whose text matches the tree (correct).
* What it must never do
- Never silence the lint; it keeps printing until the gap is closed.
- Never fix a crossing by adding a require in the other direction.
* Tests
- the one-way stage's own output is the test.
* Docs it updates
- =plan/ARCHITECTURE.org= (if corrected), =plan/OPEN-DECISIONS.org=
  (the question is answered), =plan/PROGRESS.org=.
* Depends on new components
- none.
* Build order
- After the owner answers; before other new components land, so they
  inherit a law that is true.
